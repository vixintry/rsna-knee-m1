## Pre-specified decision rule (fixed before any weak-label result exists)

Binding variant: `production` (series ladder sagittal-fluid, coronal-fluid, sagittal-any, coronal-any, axial).

- **KEEP** if weak-trained macro AUC on gold >= **0.68** AND the 95% bootstrap CI of the paired difference has a lower bound > 0.
- **INVESTIGATE** if the point estimate beats gold-only CV but either condition fails.
- **REJECT** otherwise.

Where 0.68 comes from: it is about +0.065 over the recorded 0.616, roughly two of my *estimated* standard errors (0.03-0.04, not measured) for macro AUC on 58 studies. It is a judgement call. Change it **before** running, never after.

`sagittal_only` (training and gold restricted to studies with a sagittal fluid-sensitive series) is a sensitivity check and does not decide anything.

## Known confounds, stated up front

- **Label definition drift.** Weak labels follow the parser's reading of Spanish reports; gold labels follow the explicit annotation criteria (e.g. effusion is moderate/large only). Per-target gaps may be definition mismatch, not model quality.
- **Gold informed the parser.** EXP-007 reviewed parser/gold disagreements, so parser agreement on gold is optimistic. This does **not** contaminate model fitting (gold never enters training), but it means weak-label quality on non-gold studies is probably lower than the gold agreement suggests.
- **Population shift.** The 58 gold studies look enriched (fracture 31%, effusion 60%, ACL 41%). The script prints gold vs weak prevalence. A large gap means gold AUC is a poor proxy for the hidden test.
- **Plane mixing.** Production's ladder trains and tests across planes with a sagittal-fitted scaler. Rung counts for gold and weak sets are reported. Fixing this is EXP-013, not part of this experiment.
- **One holdout of 58.** The bootstrap CI will be wide. A "REJECT" or "INVESTIGATE" is weaker evidence than a "KEEP", and none of it is leaderboard evidence (Gate C).
- **Only the reports' findings that are visible in the one chosen plane can be learned.** Some weak-label noise is therefore structural.
